## Security ruling
The replay key must be stable across semantically equivalent x402 envelopes. Raw proof bytes remain only a secondary evidence digest; they are not sufficient as the primary payment identity because harmless envelope changes can alter the proof string without changing the Nano block being settled.

The local identity does not replace facilitator verification or Nano settlement. Verification and settlement remain authoritative for payment validity.
